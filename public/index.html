Connect a YouTube Music account

Sign in at YouTube Music with the account you want to use. Open developer tools → Network, click Library, and select a successful /browse request. Copy its request headers, including Cookie and Authorization. You can paste raw headers, Chrome’s “Copy as fetch (Node.js)”, or browser.json JSON below.

These headers grant access to your account. They stay on this computer. No Google developer app is required. Connection guide
