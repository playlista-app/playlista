Save your playlists outside your streaming service

Playlista imports playlists from Spotify or YouTube Music and saves them as text files on your computer. You can change providers without rebuilding your playlists.

  • Import & export
  • Nested playlists & tags
  • Plain text files
  • Optional GitHub backup
